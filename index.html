Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you apply.

These answers cover the most common concerns people have before they commit to coaching.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the coaching path and timeline that fits your goals best. I focus on application-first qualification instead of posting generic pricing that ignores how much support you actually need.

How long should I expect to work with you?

That depends on your starting point and goal, but real transformation usually takes longer than people want to admit. I coach for sustainable progress, not crash-change expectations.

Do I get customized workouts?

Yes. I build every program around your current level, weak points, target physique, and whether you're focused on lifestyle transformation, growth, prep, or posing.

Do you help with nutrition too?

Yes. Nutrition support is built into the system, because training hard without diet alignment leaves too much progress on the table.

What if meal prep is one of my biggest problems?

That's something I focus on directly. Meal prep and nutrition adherence are where most people fall off, so I build coaching plans to make that side easier to actually follow.

Is this only for competitors?

No. I coach both lifestyle clients who want an aesthetic transformation and competitors who need prep, posing, or a longer off-season growth plan.

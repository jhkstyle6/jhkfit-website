Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before you apply.

These answers cover the most common concerns people have before they commit to coaching.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the coaching path and timeline that best fit your goals. The current site focuses on application-first qualification rather than posting generic pricing that ignores the level of support needed.

How long should I expect to work with JHKFIT?

That depends on your starting point and goal, but real transformation usually needs a longer horizon than people want to admit. JHKFIT is designed around sustainable progress, not crash-change expectations.

Do I get customized workouts?

Yes. Programs are built around your current level, weak points, target physique, and whether your focus is lifestyle transformation, growth, prep, or posing.

Do you help with nutrition too?

Yes. Nutrition support is part of the system because training hard without diet alignment leaves too much progress on the table.

What if meal prep is one of my biggest problems?

That is a real focus area for JHKFIT. Meal prep and nutrition adherence are often where clients fall off, so the coaching direction is built to make that side easier to follow in real life.

Is this only for competitors?

No. JHKFIT supports both lifestyle clients who want an aesthetic transformation and competitors who need prep, posing, or a longer off-season growth plan.
